Let's add two new commands.  The first new command, "dock", allows a ship to dock at a starbase or planet.  Ships are now required to dock at a starbase or planet in order to for the ship to receive repairs from teh starbase or planet.  Ships docked at a starbase will have its damage repaired at a rate of up to 10 percent per turn.  The rate of repair provided by the starbase itself is proportional to the amount of damage the starbase has.  For example, if the starbase's damage is at 50 percent, it can only on repair the ship at the rate of 5 percent per turn instead of 10 percent.  The ship can issue a repair command by itself without additional parameters on top the auto repair received from the starbase per turn.  So if the starbase is able to provide a repair rate of 5 percent per turn, the ship can issue a repair command to achieve a combined repair rate of 10 percent per turn.  This applies to NPC ships as well as the player.

Ships can only be resupplied with torpedos and fuel while docked at a starbase.  Existing game rules for the rate of resupply should be preserved.  The previous 1 AU requirement for repairs and resupply no longer applies as the ship is required to dock for these benefits.

Ships docked at a planet can have 10 percent of the ship's damage repaired per turn.  Ships also need to be docked at a habited planet in order to replensish its crew.  The number of crew members received from the player is between 0 and 1000 each time the ship is docked.  The rate of replenshing crew is once per dock and not per turn.

Ships can only dock again at a starbase or planet after 5 turns has elapse since the last dock.

The second new command is "undock" which allows the ship to detach from the starbase or planet
When docked at a starbase or planet, the ship is removed from the 2d map if it is within senor range and can longer be attacked by other ships.  Weapons lock or auto-navigation held on the ship that is docking will be lost.  When the ship undocks from the starbase or planet, the ship will reappear next to the starbase or planet at a distance of 0.5AU.  When the ship is undocked, other ships will be able to lock their weapons or auto-navigate to it, resuming normal game mechanics of the ship.
