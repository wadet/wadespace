When either the player's ship or an NPC ship sustains more than 50 percent damage, there is a 25% chance that one of the ship's seven major systems can be disabled if the ship takes additional damage in subsequent turns.  The seven major ship systems that can be disabled due to additional damage are:  shields, engines, torpedoes, phasers, scanners, radios, and computers:
1) If the shields are disabled, the shields are then ineffective until repaired.  Further damage inflected on the ship should be treated as if the shields are down.  If this happens to the player's ship, a message should be displayed to the player in the message area to tell the player that the shields are inoperative due to damage to the player's ship when it occurs.   If the player attempts to raise the shields after the system has been disabled, a message should be shown to the player to inform the player that the shields are inoperative due to damage to the ship.
2) If the engines are disabled, the ship should come to a full stop and can longer move until its engines are repaired.  If this happens to the player's ship, a message should be displayed to the player in the message area to tell the player that the engines are inoperative due to damage to the player's ship when it occurs.  If the player attempts to navigate or engage the warp or impluse drives after the system has been disabled, a message should be shown to the player to inform the player that the engines are inoperative due to damage to the ship.
3) If the torpedos are disabled, the ship can no longer fire torpedos until repaired.  If this happens to the player's ship, a message should be displayed to the player in the message area to tell the player that the torpedos are inoperative due to damage to the player's ship when it occurs.  If the player attempts to fire torpedos after the system has been disabled, a message should be shown to the player to inform the player that the torpedos are inoperative due to damage to the ship.
4) If the phasers are disabled, the ship can no longer fire phasers.  If this happens to the player's ship, a message should be displayed to the player in the message area to tell the player that the phasers are inoperative due to damage to the player's ship when it occurs.  If the player attempts to fire phasers after the system has been disabled, a message should be shown to the player to inform the player that the phasers are inoperative due to damage to the ship.
5) If the scanners are disabled, objects in the 2d map should not be shown until the scanners are repaired.  If this happens to the player's ship, a message should be displayed in the center of the 2d map that says "Scanners are inoperative due to damage" when it occurs.  If the player attempts to use the scanners after the system has been disabled, a message should be shown to the player to inform the player that the scanners are inoperative due to damage to the ship.
6) If the radios are disabled, the tell command should no longer function and the ship can longer receive messages from other objects until the radios are repaired.  If this happens to the player's ship, a message should be displayed to the player in the message area to tell the player that the radios are inoperative due to damage to the player's ship when it occurs.  If the player attempts to use the radios after the system has been disabled, a message should be shown to the player to inform the player that the radios are inoperative due to damage to the ship.
7) If the ship's computers are disabled, hal, targets, lock, and nav commands will no longer function and are disabled.  If there is an existing weapons lock, the lock is cancelled and the ship can longer fire its weapons against the target object.  In addition if the ship is already auto-navigating towards a destination object, the auto-navigation is then cancelled due to being disabled but the ship should continue on its current heading and speed w/o auto heading corrections.  If this happens to the player's ship, a message should be displayed to the player in the message area to tell the player that the computers are inoperative due to damage to the player's ship when it occurs.  If the player attempts to use hal, targets, lock, and nav commands or after the system has been disabled, a message should be shown to the player to inform the player that the computers are inoperative due to damage to the ship.

Each of the seven major systems have a 25% of being repaired each turn while the ship's damage is greater than or equal to 50%.  Only one major system can be repaired per turn.  Once the ship's damage level is below 50%, the chance for a system to be repaired increases to 50% each turn.  Please display a message in the message area when any of the systems are repaired each turn.
